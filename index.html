Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Do you offer cashback?

Yes. Cashback is available with card purchases of £5 or more.

Do you accept card payments?

Yes. We accept all major debit and credit cards, along with cash payments.

Do you accept American Express?

Yes. American Express is accepted in store.

Is there public parking nearby?

Yes. There are several public car parks within a short walking distance of the store.

Can I call to check if something is in stock?

Absolutely. Call us on 01626 873426 and we'll be happy to check before you visit.

Do you sell National Lottery tickets?

Yes. National Lottery tickets and scratchcards are available in store.

Can I send or return parcels from the store?

Yes. We offer Royal Mail, UPS and Evri parcel drop-off and returns during our normal opening hours.

Do you sell newspapers and magazines?

Yes. We stock a range of daily newspapers and a selection of popular magazines.

Do you sell tobacco and vaping products?

Yes. Tobacco and vaping products are available to customers aged 18 and over.

Do you offer free local delivery?

Yes. We offer free local delivery within Shaldon. Please call us to arrange your order.

Can't find what you're looking for?

Speak to a member of our team. If we don't currently stock a particular item, we'll always do our best to help or recommend a suitable alternative.
