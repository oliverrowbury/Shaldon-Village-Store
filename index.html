Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Do you offer free local delivery?

Yes. Free local delivery is available within Shaldon. Call the store on 01626 873426 to ask about ordering and delivery availability.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 7:30am until 8:00pm, and on Sunday from 7:30am until 7:00pm.

Do you stock local Devon products?

Yes. We stock a selection of locally produced Devon goods alongside trusted national brands and everyday essentials.

Do you sell alcoholic beverages?

Yes. We stock a selection of beers, wines, spirits and other alcoholic drinks. Availability may vary.

Can I call to check whether something is in stock?

Yes. Call us on 01626 873426 and the team will be happy to help with product availability.

How can I contact the shop?

You can call 01626 873426 or email shop@shaldonvillagestore.co.uk .
